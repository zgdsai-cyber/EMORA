# Emotional Model

The product models or estimates emotional states. It does not detect, measure, or claim access to a person's true emotion.

Future phases may combine psychological equations, machine-learning predictions, contextual reasoning, temporal dynamics, and emotional memory. Those mechanisms are intentionally absent from Phase 1. Any future model revision must describe its assumptions, inputs, output ranges, and validation limits.

Predictions should include uncertainty or confidence as part of the result. Confidence is a property of the model output, not proof that the estimate is correct. Scientific validation is not claimed by this foundation.
